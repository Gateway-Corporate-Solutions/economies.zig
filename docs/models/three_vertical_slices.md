# Three vertical-slice specifications

## Cobb-Douglas exchange

For utility `u=x^α y^(1-α)`, Marshallian demands are `x=αm/pₓ` and `y=(1-α)m/pᵧ`. Validation requires budget exhaustion, positive prices, Walras' law, and aggregate excess demand below `1e-8`.

## Solow-Swan growth

Effective-worker capital follows `k'= [s k^α + (1-δ)k] / [(1+n)(1+g)]`. The fixture compares a 2,000-period transition with `k*=[s/(δ+n+g)]^(1/(1-α))` within three percent, allowing for the discrete/continuous denominator convention.

## Buy-and-hold backtest

An order becomes eligible only after its triggering event. Cash equals initial cash minus fills and fees plus sales and dividends. Splits conserve position value at the pre/post reference price. The hand fixture buys ten shares, receives a dividend, applies a two-for-one split, and liquidates.
